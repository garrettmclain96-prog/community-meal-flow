## Rules

- Personalize by stakeholder type; do not mass-blast generic copy.
- Do not imply partnership, endorsement, nonprofit status, tax deductibility, or live payment readiness unless verified.
- Payments remain test mode until production gates are cleared.
- Prefer email-first, self-service interaction. Do not make a call or meeting the default next step.
- Automated cold outreach receives at most one unanswered follow-up.
- Explicit declines / unsubscribe requests end outreach immediately.
- Way West Grill receives no additional unsolicited automated outreach unless Way West replies first or Garrett explicitly changes the rule.
- Prefer a small number of high-fit local relationships over vanity send volume.

### Messaging strategy used

**Food-system organizations:** Position ProvisionLoop as connective infrastructure around existing operators, not a replacement. Ask for workflow feedback and a practical pilot path.

**Kitchens:** Emphasize operator-controlled capacity, $0 pilot platform fee, test-mode payments, and no current obligation to process live orders. Route interested operators into the self-service kitchen intake rather than requiring a sales call.

**Civic/network connectors:** Ask for feedback, introductions, and correct channels—not endorsement or funding.

**Media:** Pitch the systems problem and build-in-public pilot honestly. Explicitly state that payments remain test mode and the real local network is still being recruited.

## Current automated follow-up policy

- Initial email: personalized by target type.
- If no meaningful human reply after at least 2 business days: exactly one short, low-pressure follow-up.
- The follow-up routes the recipient to the correct async ProvisionLoop surface; it does not ask for a call.
- No second unanswered automated follow-up.
- Bounces, generic auto-routing responses, explicit declines and unsubscribes are not followed up.
- Routine positive replies may be answered automatically with the correct self-service link.
- Contracts, legal/data terms, money/pricing, grants/procurement, press interviews/quotes, operator-authority evidence, specific meeting times, or commitments escalate to Garrett.
