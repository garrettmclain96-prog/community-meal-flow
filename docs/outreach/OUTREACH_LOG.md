# ProvisionLoop Outreach Log

Durable record of direct outreach tied to the Galveston County pilot.

## Rules

- Personalize by stakeholder type; do not mass-blast generic copy.
- Do not imply partnership, endorsement, nonprofit status, tax deductibility, or live payment readiness unless verified.
- Payments remain test mode until production gates are cleared.
- Log replies, meetings, objections, referrals, and next follow-up dates here.
- Prefer a small number of high-fit local relationships over vanity send volume.

### Messaging strategy used

**Food-system organizations:** Position ProvisionLoop as connective infrastructure around existing operators, not a replacement. Ask for workflow feedback and a practical pilot conversation.

**Kitchens:** Emphasize operator-controlled capacity, $0 pilot platform fee, test-mode payments, and no current obligation to process live orders. Ask them to pressure-test the workflow before considering pilot participation.

**Civic/network connectors:** Ask for feedback, introductions, and correct channels—not endorsement or funding.

**Media:** Pitch the systems problem and build-in-public pilot honestly. Explicitly state that payments remain test mode and the real local network is still being recruited.

## Follow-up cadence

- Day 0: initial email sent.
- Day 3–4: short follow-up if no reply.
- Day 7–10: one final useful follow-up with a concrete ask or new proof point.
- After reply: log owner, response summary, next action, and date.
