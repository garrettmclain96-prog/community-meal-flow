# ProvisionLoop movement standard

## North Star

**I won't let good intentions fail at execution.**

ProvisionLoop should never ask for credit because somebody cared, donated, registered, mapped a resource, or clicked a button. Credit starts when the outcome is real.

## Brand architecture

- **Movement:** good intentions must reach the other end.
- **System:** ProvisionLoop.
- **Unit of proof:** one completed, recorded loop.
- **Public scoreboard:** verified aggregate closed-loop outcomes.
- **Call to action:** close the next loop.

## Voice

Direct. Honest. Operational. Human.

Write like somebody fixing a broken handoff, not somebody selling a civic-tech platform.

Avoid nonprofit theater, vague inspiration, corporate-speak, projected impact, and generic SaaS language.

Preferred language is concrete: meal, kitchen, request, funding, delivery, handoff, proof, tonight, local, verified, recorded, completed.

## Non-negotiables

1. Every public number is backed by actual recorded data or explicitly labeled as an estimate.
2. No projected meal totals are presented as impact.
3. No fictional testimonials, partners, kitchens, recipient stories, or success snapshots.
4. A directory listing is not an affiliation claim.
5. A payment is not a completed meal.
6. Recipient identity stays private unless explicit consent and the product's privacy rules permit a specific use.
7. Every CTA asks for one clear action.
8. When the proof does not exist yet, say so plainly.

## Visual direction

ProvisionLoop should feel like local working infrastructure: dispatch board, kitchen ticket, proof of delivery, field operations, stamped status, hard borders, timestamps, real data, and one action accent.

It should not look like a generic nonprofit donation template or generic SaaS landing page.

Use real operational photography only when provenance and consent are known. Until a verified real image is available, a truthful data/status surface is stronger than stock imagery.

## Core public lines

- **Good intentions need execution.**
- **No outcome. No credit.**
- **Caring isn't a delivery system.**
- **If help never reaches the other end, the job is not finished.**
- **Close the next loop.**

## Proof behavior

The homepage proof module should automatically show the latest verified delivery when the public data model supports one. Before the first verified delivery exists, it should remain visibly empty rather than substituting a demo success story.

The civic ledger remains the detailed public proof surface. Recipient-level information never becomes public proof merely to make the story more emotional.
